## CARD
goal: An escaped-defect prevention becomes policy only after later independent evidence shows it works
why: `fold --bind` currently checks that a prevention address resolves, so the Task that files an escape may immediately turn its proposed remedy into a binding decision without any later execution of that remedy.
next: seal scope and receipt-purpose authority first, then freeze the architecture contract for later validation.

## SCOPE
In: escape `fold --bind` only; an explicit later validation receipt; distinct Task and committed Git order; exact prevention-file scope/blob containment; the later Task's bound receipt and PASS gate cite the same evidence.
Out: changing plain lessons, ordinary fold, `--reject`, reinterpreting dates as order, or automatically inventing a policy from a finding.

## GROUND
depends-on: seal-what-you-signed · evidence-has-a-purpose
risks:
  - current prevention refs prove existence rather than effectiveness
  - receipt dates have day resolution and cannot prove later ordering
  - receipt purpose must be bound before it can authorize policy
  - scope must be sealed before a later Task's claimed ownership can be trusted

## EXIT
- [ ] E1 `fold --bind` on an escape requires a distinct later Task's committed descendant, fresh bound green receipt and PASS gate over the exact prevention file/blob; stale, self, date-only or uncited evidence refuses atomically   (← prevention-earns-binding)

## CLOSE
evidence: targeted receipt and fresh T2 challenge after A scope seal and C1 receipt purpose are gated.
