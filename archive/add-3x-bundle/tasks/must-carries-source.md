## RULES
<must>
- M1 a Must line may end with `(from: <ref> · fails-on: <text>)`; `<ref>` is free text naming where the rule was told (a person, `interview`, a spec address, a ticket) and `fails-on:` names the plausible wrong reading a check must fail on; either half may stand alone; the tail is part of the Must's text and so of the direction digest — a sourced Must reads exactly as an unsourced one to every existing reader
- M2 `interview` on a Task compiles every Must with no `from:` as a decision `{id: M<n>, of: must, dim: source, reading: <the Must>}` AFTER assumptions, edges and rejects; `confirm` on it rewrites the line in place appending `(from: interview)` (or adds `from: interview` into an existing tail that carries only `fails-on:`); `correct` and `defer` write nothing to the line; a Must already carrying `from:` is never asked
- M3 `freeze` on a rung-bound task appends `notice: M2, M5 carry no from: — a Must is what you were told; add interview <slug> or write (from: …)` to its success line when any Must lacks `from:` — a notice, never a refusal (R:SOURCEASREFUSAL); at process, quick or explore nothing is said
- M4 `interview_gap` (the freeze's interviewed-human check) counts an unanswered Must decision exactly as an unanswered assumption — a human-floor freeze with an unsourced Must never interviewed refuses as it refuses an un-interviewed assumption today
- M5 direction.md's RULES bullet names the tail and what `fails-on:` is for; the skill surface stays line-neutral vs HEAD and the three trees identical
</must>
<reject>
- R:SOURCEASREFUSAL a plan-or-process freeze refusing on an unsourced Must — the tail is a notice everywhere but the human interview -> "SOURCEASREFUSAL"
- R:SOURCEJUDGED the engine deciding whether `from:` is true, or resolving it — it is recorded as handed -> "SOURCEJUDGED"
- R:DIGESTDRIFT a `from:` tail excluded from the direction digest so a sourced and an unsourced Must seal alike -> "DIGESTDRIFT"
</reject>
