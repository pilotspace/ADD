**Guard:** tune on a random, outcome-blind dev third (101 issues, `benchmark/runs-swe/split.json`,
seed 7, a third per repo); claim from the 199 held-out issues. Budget $150; every held-out or full
run needs the owner's OK. Harness: `--testenv docker`, official harness, `claude-sonnet-5-5`.

## Finding that reframed the track

At `--effort low`, the full-300 ADD arm read SKILL.md in only 134 of 270 runs (32 of 32 at medium).
ADD trailed vanilla both when it read the skill and when it did not (by 12 to 2 issues, p = 0.013,
when it did not), so the skill text was not what lost. Effort and the harness prompt were the
suspects.

## Verdicts

- **Effort decides the sign.** ADD at medium leads fresh vanilla by 3 on dev; at low it trails by 4.
- **Injecting the skill at low hurt** (70): more context at low effort does not help. Dropped.
- **L2 and L3 bought nothing over plain medium.** Dropped. The levers that remain are effort and
  the `maintainer-intent` skill.
- **p = 0.55 on dev is consistent with a tie.** The held-out 199 decides it. Paired with these dev
  runs, it also completes a fresh full 300 for both arms (≈ $51). That run is waiting on the owner.
