## What this says about writing a method for a model

The fixes that landed are the ones that **produce an artifact the model must write** — a check
with a named input (M2), and in round 4 a `covers:` line per rule and a `found:` line. The ones that
did not are **advice inside prose**: a parenthetical on how to guess (M3), an ordering for the
report (M4), and how to batch tool calls (M1). A model follows the shape of what it has to write
more reliably than a sentence about how to think.

Two more arm-level differences showed up this round, both in ADD's favour and neither about code
quality: **vanilla wrote no tests at all in 2 of 6 runs** (ADD: tests in 6 of 6), and **vanilla
halted on the contradictory spec in 1 of 3 amb1 runs** (ADD: 0). For unattended work, "always
delivers, always leaves tests and a record of its guesses" is the method's floor.
